**Description:**
Swap cute zoo animals, match three or more, and watch the cascades roll!

Floodlings is a cheerful match-3 puzzle with no lives and no energy timers - play as long as you like, whenever you like.

FOUR WAYS TO PLAY
• Levels - beat the target score before you run out of moves and earn up to 3 stars per level
• Species Rescue - free the glowing animals in a jungle rescue mission
• Zen Mode - no clock, no move limit, no way to fail. Just relax and watch flowers bloom
• Time Attack - score as much as you can before the clock runs out

SHINY, SATISFYING COMBOS
• Chain cascades for NICE!, GREAT!... all the way up to LEGENDARY!
• Build hot streaks and set the board on fire
• Sparkling effects, bouncy animals and a score that counts up with every match

YOUR PROGRESS, SAVED
• Pick up right where you left off
• Collect stars and track your personal bests in every mode

ALSO
• Light and dark themes
• Plays offline
• Free to play, supported by ads
